    Navigated brain tumour removal

    A guided simulation on real BraTS MRI, done in the navigation view. The navigation is aligned to the patient's head, then you open the head and remove the tumour while it tracks your instrument against the brain, the tumour and the skull from the MRI.

    1. PlanCheck the automatic approach and approve it.
    2. AlignScan the face so the navigation matches the patient.
    3. OpenMark, cut the scalp, drill, lift the bone, open the dura.
    4. RemoveAspirate the tumour. Watch the slices and the warnings.
    5. ReviewExtent of resection, safety and accuracy.
    Mouse
    moves the instrument over the patient; hold the left button on the patient to use it
    Rotate
    right-drag, or left-drag away from the patient: the patient turns the way the mouse moves (Upright or Free, in the Layers tab)
    Zoom
    the zoom bar on the right of the 3D view, the + and − keys, or the mouse wheel
    Pan
    the hand button above the zoom bar (or H), then drag anywhere; or middle-drag, Shift + drag, Space + drag or the arrow keys · the reset button brings the view back
    Undo
    Ctrl+Z, and Ctrl+Y to redo, or the arrow buttons at the top right: every step of the procedure
    Look
    Hologram or Opaque (the real scalp and skull) at the top left of the 3D view, with a transparency slider for each
    F · I · M
    full screen · focus mode (hide panels) · maximise the 3D view
    Esc
    leave focus mode or a maximised view

    Every step has a Do it for me button if you only want to watch.
