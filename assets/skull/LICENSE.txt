Skull model (skull_s1397.png: the PNG's RGB bytes carry the mesh data)

Derived from subject s1397 of the TotalSegmentator dataset, version 2.0.1:
  https://zenodo.org/records/10047292
  Wasserthal J. et al. TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic
  Structures in CT Images. Radiology: Artificial Intelligence, 2023.
  https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.230024

Changes made:
- the bone surface was extracted from the CT around the skull segmentation,
  smoothed and simplified into three levels of detail (about 90,000, 26,000 and 9,000
  triangles);
- it was shaded with ambient occlusion computed from the CT and a cavity term
  from the surface curvature;
- it was warped (by a skin-to-skin registration) to sit under the
  "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" face used by CranioNav, and pushed at least 1.5 mm
  inside that skin where it would otherwise show through (eye corners, nose);
- in the app, its vault is further fitted to each case's estimated skull and
  the bone is kept clear of the case's brain.
